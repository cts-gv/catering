PLACE YOUR HERO VIDEO HERE
===========================

Put your video file in this folder and name it: hero-video.mp4

The website will automatically use it as the background video in the hero section.

Supported formats: .mp4 (recommended), .webm

To use a different filename or format:
  1. Open src/config/site.ts
  2. Find the "hero" section
  3. Change videoSrc to "/your-filename.webm" etc.

To use a different poster image (shown before video loads):
  1. Open src/config/site.ts
  2. Find the "hero" section
  3. Change posterImage to "/your-image.jpg" or a URL

Tips for a great hero video:
  - Keep it 10-30 seconds long (it loops automatically)
  - Use 1920x1080 or similar resolution
  - Muted is fine (the video plays without sound)
  - Show cooking action, tacos being made, or event scenes
